Company Configuration — Delivery Tickets

Control how delivery tickets are issued across the business. The Printed Ticket Fallback lets dispatch or plant supervisors switch to manually printed tickets when the network, carrier signal, or Deliveries service is unavailable.

Printed Ticket Fallback

Primary company-wide switch. When enabled, selected plants may print physical tickets to hand to the driver instead of waiting on the digital workflow.

Enable Printed Ticket Fallback

Allows authorised users to switch one or more plants into manual (printed) ticketing. Required before any plant-level override will take effect.

Supervisor mode lets the batchman flip the switch locally during an outage without waiting on dispatch.
Printed tickets use a distinct prefix so they can be reconciled when the system is back online. Default: M-.
Reserve a block of numbers per plant to avoid collisions.

Require re-entry when network returns

Each manual ticket appears in the Sync Queue so a user confirms, matches it to an order and records the final delivery into Deliveries.

Auto-exit fallback when network restored

Automatically switch plants back to digital ticketing once the Deliveries heartbeat is healthy for 10 minutes.

Audit log all transitions

Record who toggled fallback on/off, at which plant, and why. Visible in the Sync Queue and Admin audit log.
